Heart Disease Multivariate Dashboard

Linked views for browsing, highlighting, filtering, and patient comparison (4DV806 Assignment 3).

Tips:
  • Click a bar in “Outcome overview” to filter all views.
  • Use sliders to filter age range; use dropdown for sex.
  • In scatterplot: choose X/Y variables, drag to brush, click points to select patients.
  • Selected patients appear in “Patient details”.
